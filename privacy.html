Privacy policy

Last updated 11 November 2026.

Cuaderno is a personal app. It runs on its owner's own computer and is not offered as a service to anyone else.

What it reads from Google

  • Calendar events (read-only): titles, times and locations of upcoming events, to start and stop lesson recordings. Events are never changed.
  • Drive files: only files the app created itself (backups), and, read-only, recordings placed in its own inbox folder by the companion iPhone app.

Where data goes

Everything is stored on the owner's computer. Sign-in tokens are encrypted there. Google data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Audio and transcripts are sent to a speech-to-text service and a language model only to produce the owner's own transcripts and notes.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
