Cuaderno

Cuaderno is a personal lecture notetaker. It runs on its owner's own computer, records lessons in Greek, Spanish and English, transcribes them, and turns them into study notes and a homework list.

Google access

Cuaderno asks for narrow Google permissions, each used only by the app's owner:

  • Google Calendar, read-only: to know when lessons start and end, so recording can start and stop by itself.
  • Google Drive, files this app creates: to back up its own documents and recordings.
  • Google Drive, read-only: to receive recordings made by the companion iPhone app.

See the privacy policy.
